How to use

  1. Pasta first — Place the measured dry pasta in the cooker.
  2. Cold water — Add the shown volume. Pasta must be fully covered.
  3. Salt — Add the matching salt (≈ 9 g per litre).
  4. Stir once — Cook uncovered on High.
  5. Mid-cook stir — Halfway through, open carefully and stir (especially long pasta).
  6. Test early — Check 10 s before the displayed time. Add 10 s steps to al dente.
  7. Drain — Use the strainer lid. Stand 1 min for better texture.

How to use

  1. Measure — 1 Reishunger becher ≈ 150 g dry rice (2 portions). Max 450 g (3 becher).
  2. Rinse — Jasmine and basmati twice; koshihikari 3–4 times until the water is only faintly cloudy. Drain well.
  3. Water — Add the shown cold tap water. Optional 10 min soak for Daawat basmati, then cook in the same water.
  4. Salt — Jasmine and basmati: the shown pinch. Koshihikari: none in the cooker (season after with sushi-zu).
  5. Lid on — Close the rice cooker so the lid vents can circulate steam. Never run it dry.
  6. High — Full microwave power. Lid on, no grill. Glance once that water remains.
  7. Rest — Leave the lid on for the shown rest. Then fluff with the paddle.

How to use

  1. Cut even — Same-size pieces. Times assume the cut listed on the vegetable.
  2. Vessel — Pasta cooker with a little water, or any microwave-safe bowl with a vented lid. The Reishunger rice cooker is not a steamer.
  3. Water — Only the shown splash. The vegetables steam in their own moisture plus that film of water.
  4. High — Covered, full microwave power. No grill. Stir or shake halfway on portions over 250 g.
  5. Stop early — Crisp-tender. Rest 1 min covered, then uncover so they do not keep cooking.

How to use

  1. Combination — select the mode named on the readout.
  2. Rack — High rack on the glass tray for pieces, toast, pizza, chips. Heat-proof dish on the turntable for gratins and lasagna.
  3. Not in plastic — Do not put the pasta cooker or the Reishunger rice cooker under the grill.
  4. Turn — Halfway on meat, sausages, chips, toast. Fat will spit; use gloves.
  5. Why which mode — grill-heavy for thin or already cooked food, more microwave for dense or frozen.

Algorithm

Fitted for the selected microwave. LG NeoChef MH6535 at 1000 W (P_eff 650 W); AEG MFB295DB at 900 W (P_eff 585 W). Cold tap ≈ 15 °C, uncovered cooker.

t = t_ad + (1 − γ) · t_heat + t_die
  • t_ad = t_pkg − 1:00 (al dente from the box number; floor 75% of package)
  • t_heat = (V·c_w·ΔT_w + m_p·c_p·ΔT_p) / P_eff using the actual sliders
  • c_w = 4.184 J/g·K, ΔT_w = 82 K (15 °C tap → ~97 °C)
  • c_p = 1.8 J/g·K, ΔT_p = 70 K
  • P_eff — 650 W on the LG (1000 W rating), 585 W on the AEG (900 W rating). ~65% coupling in a deep cooker.
  • γ = 0.29 ordinary, 0.66 bronze
  • t_die bronze: max(0, 5.31 + 0.58·(t_pkg − 11)) (284 g / 902 ml Spaghetti al Bronzo → 18:20; 250 g / 834 ml Spaghettoni → 19:40; 154 g / 617 ml → 17:20)
  • t_die ordinary: max(0, 1.11·(t_pkg − 8)) — longer box numbers need more still-cooker lag (333 g / 1 l / 8:00 → 14:00; 250 g / 834 ml Barilla Spaghetti 10:00 → 17:00)
Result rounded to the nearest 10 s.

Algorithm

Fitted to the Reishunger microwave rice cooker (model 546-W), lid on, plus variety offsets. Booklet times were 750–800 W. Heat-up uses the selected microwave’s P_eff (650 W on the LG, 585 W on the AEG).

t = t_gel + (1 − γ) · t_heat + t_bulk
  • t_gel — time at cooking temperature: jasmine 8.5 · basmati 9.5 · koshihikari 10.0 min
  • t_heat = (V·c_w·ΔT_w) / P_eff with the selected microwave’s P_eff
  • γ = 0.22 — steam under the lid already cooks during heat-up
  • t_bulk = 0.35·(n−1)² min, n = m_rice / 150 g — crowding in the 1.7 l cooker
  • Water follows the manual cup table (1→1.5, 2→3, 3→4) times a variety factor: jasmine 1.00 · basmati 1.08 · koshihikari 0.78
Result rounded to the nearest 10 s. Then rest lid-on.

Algorithm

Full microwave power, covered. Times averaged from microwave steaming charts (Serious Eats, Whirlpool, OSU Extension, BBC Good Food) at 1000 W, then scaled by mass and by the selected microwave’s rated power.

t = t_250 · (m / 250)^0.55 + 0.15 · V / 30
  • t_250 — minutes for 250 g of that cut at 1000 W, scaled by 1000 / rated W
  • Exponent 0.55 — extra mass does not add time linearly in a microwave
  • Water is only a steam film: about 30–60 ml per 250 g
Result rounded to 10 s. Then rest 1 min covered.

Algorithm

Grill combination. LG NeoChef MH6535: Co-1 200 W + 900 W grill, Co-2 300 W, Co-3 400 W. AEG MFB295DB: Combi-Hi 36% microwave / 64% grill, Combi-Lo 55% / 45%. No preheat. High rack unless the note says dish.

t = t_base · (m / m_base)^0.65
  • t_base from grill-combi charts (chicken pieces ~18 min / 500 g at ~300 W+grill, gratins ~18 min / 450 g at ~400 W+grill, frozen pizza ~12 min / 350 g)
  • LG: Co-1 surface, Co-2 roast, Co-3 dense or frozen. AEG: Combi-Hi for surface and roast, Combi-Lo for dense or frozen
Turn at half time when the note says so.
